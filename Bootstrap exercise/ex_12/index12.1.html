Exercise 12.1 - Margin & Padding Utilities

Margin Utilities
m-3 — margin on ALL sides (1rem)
mt-4 — margin TOP only (1.5rem)
mb-4 — margin BOTTOM only (1.5rem)
ms-5 — margin START/LEFT only (3rem)
me-5 — margin END/RIGHT only (3rem)
mx-auto — center horizontally
my-4 — margin TOP & BOTTOM (1.5rem)
Padding Utilities
p-2 — padding on ALL sides (0.5rem)
p-4 — padding on ALL sides (1.5rem)
py-5 — padding TOP & BOTTOM (3rem)
px-5 — padding LEFT & RIGHT (3rem)
pt-4 — padding TOP only (1.5rem)
pb-4 — padding BOTTOM only (1.5rem)
